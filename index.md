# SuBjEcT Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 12 September 2026**

This Privacy Policy applies generally to applications, software, and digital services created, published, maintained, or managed by **SuBjEcT** (“SuBjEcT”, “we”, “us”, or “our”).

SuBjEcT respects the privacy of its users and follows a privacy-conscious approach to application development. Our general principle is to minimise the collection and processing of personal information and only request access to information or device capabilities where necessary to provide application functionality.

## Information We Collect

Unless specifically disclosed for a particular application or service, SuBjEcT does not intentionally collect, store, sell, rent, or share personally identifiable information.

Our applications may store information locally on a user's device, including preferences, settings, favourites, history, progress, configuration information, or other information necessary for application functionality.

Information stored solely on the user's device is not automatically transmitted to or accessible by SuBjEcT.

Where an application requires information to be transmitted or processed externally, this will be disclosed where appropriate.

## Device Permissions

Some SuBjEcT applications may request access to device capabilities such as location, notifications, camera, microphone, photos or media, files or storage, network connectivity, background services, or other operating-system functionality.

Permissions are requested only where they are required or useful for the application's intended functionality.

Granting a device permission does not necessarily mean that SuBjEcT collects, receives, or stores the information accessible through that permission.

Users can normally manage or revoke permissions through their device's system settings. Revoking a permission may prevent the associated feature from operating correctly.

## Location Information

Certain applications may use location information where location is necessary for their functionality.

Where location information is processed locally on the user's device and is not transmitted to SuBjEcT, we do not receive or maintain a record of that location.

Applications that transmit, synchronise, or otherwise externally process location information will provide appropriate disclosure where required.

## Locally Stored Information

SuBjEcT applications may store information locally to provide features such as preferences, saved items, application history, progress, schedules, configuration, or other application-specific functionality.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, locally stored information remains on the user's device.

Users can generally remove locally stored information using application controls, clearing the application's data, or uninstalling the application.

## Third-Party Services

Some applications may interact with third-party services where necessary to provide particular functionality. These may include operating-system services, mapping providers, websites, APIs, cloud services, app stores, or other external platforms.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Where a third-party service materially affects how user information is processed, SuBjEcT will endeavour to provide appropriate disclosure.

## Advertising

Unless explicitly stated otherwise within a particular application or its store listing, SuBjEcT applications do not use personal information for targeted advertising.

If advertising services are introduced into an application, the relevant privacy disclosures will be updated accordingly.

## Sale of Personal Information

SuBjEcT does **not sell users' personal information**.

We do not operate our applications for the purpose of creating or selling profiles of users' private activities.

## Analytics and Diagnostics

Some applications or distribution platforms may provide technical information relating to application performance, crashes, installation statistics, device compatibility, or diagnostics.

Where analytics or diagnostic services are used directly by an application, they will be considered when determining the application's applicable privacy and data-safety disclosures.

## Children's Privacy

SuBjEcT takes children's privacy seriously.

Applications designed for or likely to be used by children are developed with particular consideration for privacy and data minimisation.

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children unless such processing is necessary for a clearly disclosed feature and is handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

Parents or guardians who believe personal information concerning a child has been improperly processed are encouraged to contact SuBjEcT.

## Security

SuBjEcT aims to minimise the amount of personal information processed by its applications.

Where information is stored or processed, reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the nature of the application and information are used.

However, no software, device, network, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Data Retention

Where information exists exclusively on a user's device, SuBjEcT does not independently retain that information.

If an application or service stores personal information on systems controlled by SuBjEcT, appropriate information regarding retention and deletion will be provided.

## Data Deletion

For information stored solely on the user's device, users can generally delete that information by using deletion or reset functionality within the application, clearing the application's storage/data through the operating system, or uninstalling the application.

Where SuBjEcT operates an online service that stores identifiable user information, users may contact us regarding applicable deletion requests.

## External Links

SuBjEcT applications may contain links to websites or services operated by third parties.

SuBjEcT is not responsible for the content, security, or privacy practices of independently operated third-party websites or services.

Users are encouraged to review the privacy policies of those services.

## Changes to Applications

Different SuBjEcT applications provide different functionality.

Accordingly, individual applications may require additional privacy disclosures depending on their functionality, permissions, third-party services, or future development.

Where an application's handling of information differs materially from this general policy, additional disclosure may be provided within the application, its store listing, or accompanying documentation.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

SuBjEcT may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to our applications, services, technologies, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

Updates will be published at this location and the **Last updated** date will be revised accordingly.

Users are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
