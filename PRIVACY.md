# SuBjEcT Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 21 September 2026**

This Privacy Policy applies generally to applications, software, and
digital services created, published, maintained, or managed by
**SuBjEcT** ("SuBjEcT", "we", "us", or "our").

SuBjEcT respects the privacy of its users and follows a
privacy-conscious approach to application development. Our general
principle is to minimise the collection and processing of personal
information and only request access to information or device
capabilities where necessary to provide application functionality.

## Information We Collect

Unless specifically disclosed for a particular application or service,
SuBjEcT does not intentionally collect, store, sell, rent, or share
personally identifiable information.

Our applications may store information locally on a user's device,
including preferences, settings, favourites, history, progress,
configuration information, or other information necessary for
application functionality.

Information stored solely on the user's device is not automatically
transmitted to or accessible by SuBjEcT.

Where an application requires information to be transmitted or processed
externally, this will be disclosed where appropriate.

## Device Permissions

Some SuBjEcT applications may request access to device capabilities such
as location, notifications, camera, microphone, photos or media, files
or storage, network connectivity, background services, or other
operating-system functionality.

Permissions are requested only where they are required or useful for the
application's intended functionality.

Granting a device permission does not necessarily mean that SuBjEcT
collects, receives, or stores the information accessible through that
permission.

Users can normally manage or revoke permissions through their device's
system settings. Revoking a permission may prevent the associated
feature from operating correctly.

## Location Information

Certain applications may use location information where location is
necessary for their functionality.

Where location information is processed locally on the user's device and
is not transmitted to SuBjEcT, we do not receive or maintain a record of
that location.

Applications that transmit, synchronise, or otherwise externally process
location information will provide appropriate disclosure where required.

## Locally Stored Information

SuBjEcT applications may store information locally to provide features
such as preferences, saved items, application history, progress,
schedules, configuration, or other application-specific functionality.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, locally stored information remains
on the user's device.

Users can generally remove locally stored information using application
controls, clearing the application's data, or uninstalling the
application.

## Third-Party Services

Some applications may interact with third-party services where necessary
to provide particular functionality. These may include operating-system
services, mapping providers, websites, APIs, cloud services, app stores,
or other external platforms.

Third-party services may process information according to their own
privacy policies and terms.

Where a third-party service materially affects how user information is
processed, SuBjEcT will endeavour to provide appropriate disclosure.

## Apple Platform Services

Some SuBjEcT applications are distributed through Apple's App Store and
may use Apple-provided operating-system services and frameworks.

Where an application uses Apple services such as Apple Calendar,
notifications, StoreKit, iCloud, or other Apple platform functionality,
information may be processed by Apple in accordance with Apple's
applicable privacy policies and terms.

Use of an Apple-provided framework or permission does not by itself mean
that SuBjEcT receives or stores the information handled by that
framework. Any application-specific use of these services that
materially affects how user information is handled will be described in
the relevant application section of this policy or in the application's
store disclosures.

## Advertising

Unless explicitly stated otherwise within a particular application or
its store listing, SuBjEcT applications do not use personal information
for targeted advertising.

If advertising services are introduced into an application, the relevant
privacy disclosures will be updated accordingly.

## Sale of Personal Information

SuBjEcT does **not sell users' personal information**.

We do not operate our applications for the purpose of creating or
selling profiles of users' private activities.

## Analytics and Diagnostics

Some applications or distribution platforms may provide technical
information relating to application performance, crashes, installation
statistics, device compatibility, or diagnostics.

Where analytics or diagnostic services are used directly by an
application, they will be considered when determining the application's
applicable privacy and data-safety disclosures.

## Children's Privacy

SuBjEcT takes children's privacy seriously.

Applications designed for or likely to be used by children are developed
with particular consideration for privacy and data minimisation.

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from
children unless such processing is necessary for a clearly disclosed
feature and is handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

Parents or guardians who believe personal information concerning a child
has been improperly processed are encouraged to contact SuBjEcT.

## Security

SuBjEcT aims to minimise the amount of personal information processed by
its applications.

Where information is stored or processed, reasonable technical and
organisational measures appropriate to the nature of the application and
information are used.

However, no software, device, network, or electronic storage system can
be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Data Retention

Where information exists exclusively on a user's device, SuBjEcT does
not independently retain that information.

If an application or service stores personal information on systems
controlled by SuBjEcT, appropriate information regarding retention and
deletion will be provided.

## Data Deletion

For information stored solely on the user's device, users can generally
delete that information by using deletion or reset functionality within
the application, clearing the application's storage/data through the
operating system, or uninstalling the application.

Where SuBjEcT operates an online service that stores identifiable user

## External Links

SuBjEcT applications may contain links to websites or services operated
by third parties.

SuBjEcT is not responsible for the content, security, or privacy
practices of independently operated third-party websites or services.

Users are encouraged to review the privacy policies of those services.

## Changes to Applications

Different SuBjEcT applications provide different functionality.

Accordingly, individual applications may require additional privacy
disclosures depending on their functionality, permissions, third-party

Where an application's handling of information differs materially from

## School Planner: Family

School Planner: Family is a family organisation application for planning
children's school and preschool weeks, including schedules, attendance,
school hours, clubs, lunches, PE days, childcare costs, school closures,
reminders, notes, and related planning information.

### Family and Planner Information

Information entered into School Planner: Family, such as child names,
school or preschool details, schedules, clubs, lunches, PE days, costs,
closures, reminders, preferences, and other planning information, is
currently stored locally on the user's device.

SuBjEcT does not currently operate a School Planner: Family account
system or backend database that receives or stores this planner
information.

Because some information entered into the application may relate to
children, users should only enter information that is appropriate and
necessary for their own family planning purposes. Locally stored planner
information is controlled by the user through their device and the
application's available controls.

### Apple Calendar

School Planner: Family may request permission to access Apple Calendar
so that the user can create, update, or manage calendar events relating
to their planned school week.

Calendar access is used to provide the calendar functionality requested
by the user. SuBjEcT does not operate a server that receives or stores
the user's Apple Calendar contents through this feature.

Users can manage or revoke Calendar permission through their Apple
device settings. Revoking Calendar access may prevent Calendar Sync from
operating correctly.

### Notifications and Reminders

School Planner: Family may request permission to send local
notifications, including optional reminders relating to the user's
planned school week.

Notification preferences and scheduled local reminders are handled
through the application and Apple's operating-system notification
functionality. Users can manage notification permissions through their
Apple device settings.

### Local Backup and Restore

Where School Planner: Family provides backup or restore functionality,
backup data is created or selected at the user's request. SuBjEcT does
not currently operate a School Planner: Family server to which these
backups are automatically uploaded.

If a user chooses to save, copy, share, or store a backup using an
external location or third-party service, that external service may
process the file according to its own privacy policy and terms.

### School Planner Pro and Apple Purchases

School Planner: Family may offer optional paid features through School
Planner Pro, including subscriptions or a lifetime purchase.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit.
SuBjEcT does not directly receive or store users' full payment-card
details.

Apple may process purchase, subscription, transaction, and related
account information according to Apple's applicable privacy policies and
terms.

### iCloud and Cloud Synchronisation

School Planner: Family does not currently use a SuBjEcT-operated cloud
account or backend to synchronise planner information between users or
devices.

If optional iCloud, CloudKit, shared-family synchronisation, or another
cloud synchronisation feature is introduced in a future version, the
application's privacy disclosures and this policy will be reviewed and
updated as appropriate before or alongside that functionality becoming
available.

### School Planner Data Deletion

School Planner: Family does not currently require a user account.

Locally stored School Planner: Family information can generally be
removed using available application controls or by uninstalling the
application. Information that a user has separately exported, backed up,
or added to Apple Calendar may need to be removed from the location or
service in which the user chose to store it.

## TrackMe

TrackMe is a location utility that provides location testing, route
simulation, real-device location tracking, saved locations, history and
related features.

### Location Data

TrackMe may access precise or approximate location information when the
user enables location-dependent features such as Live Location or
real-location tracking.

Tracking logs, saved locations, favourites, location history and
simulation history are primarily stored locally on the user's device.
SuBjEcT does not operate a TrackMe account system or maintain a user
database containing this location history.

### Route and Map Services

When a user requests an online road route for route simulation, selected
start and destination coordinates may be transmitted over an encrypted
HTTPS connection to a third-party routing service so that the route can
be calculated.

TrackMe may also connect to third-party mapping or content-delivery
services to provide map functionality. These third-party services may
receive standard network information, such as an IP address, as part of
normal internet communication.

### Mock Location

TrackMe uses Android's supported mock-location functionality for
legitimate testing and development purposes.

The user must explicitly select TrackMe as the mock-location application
through Android Developer Options.

TrackMe does not attempt to conceal mock-location status, bypass
application integrity systems, or prevent Android or third-party
applications from identifying a location as simulated.

### Background Location Operation

When a user explicitly starts location tracking or simulation, TrackMe
may continue the operation while the application is not visible by using
an Android foreground service.

Android displays a persistent notification while this operation is
active and provides the user with controls to stop it.

### Data Export

Users may choose to export completed tracking information to formats
such as CSV or PDF. Exported files are created at the user's request and
are controlled by the user after export.

### TrackMe Pro and Google Play Purchases

TrackMe may offer optional paid features through TrackMe Pro.

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Google Play. SuBjEcT does
not directly receive or store users' full payment-card details.

Google Play may process purchase, subscription and transaction
information according to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

### TrackMe Data Deletion

TrackMe does not currently require a user account.

Locally stored TrackMe information can be removed using available
application controls, by clearing TrackMe's application data through
Android settings, or by uninstalling the application.
